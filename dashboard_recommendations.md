# Dashboard Recommendations

## Overview

Based on the analysis, I identified several ways the student dashboard could make learner progress and engagement easier to understand.

The recommendations focus on turning the data into information that students and tutors can act on.

---

## 1. Show Weekly Learning Progress

### Recommendation

Show the student's weekly learning activity against their target.

### Suggested visualisation

A simple progress bar showing:

> 4 of 5 hours completed  
> 80% of weekly target

### Why

This allows students to quickly understand whether they are keeping up with their learning target.

---

## 2. Show Changes in Weekly Activity

### Recommendation

Show learner activity across the different weeks of the course.

### Suggested visualisation

A simple line chart showing weekly activity.

### Why

This can make changes in engagement easier to see.

For example, if activity starts to fall after Weeks 3–4, the student or tutor can identify the change earlier.

---

## 3. Highlight Inactivity

### Recommendation

Make periods of inactivity more visible.

Useful indicators could include:

- Last activity date
- Number of inactive days
- Consecutive inactive weeks

### Why

This could help tutors identify learners who may need encouragement or follow-up.

---

## 4. Show English Skills Activity

### Recommendation

Show activity across:

- Reading
- Writing
- Listening
- Speaking

### Suggested visualisation

A simple bar chart comparing activity across the four skills.

### Why

Students can quickly see which areas they have been practising.

Tutors can also use the information when reviewing learner activity.

---

## 5. Show Progress Over Time

### Recommendation

Show changes in learner performance between the middle and final assessment.

### Suggested visualisation

A simple progress indicator or comparison showing:

> Mid-course level → Final level

### Why

This makes improvement easier for students and tutors to understand.

---

## 6. Create a Clear "Next Step"

### Recommendation

Add a section that gives the student a clear action to take.

### Example

> **Your next step**  
> Complete your Speaking activity.

### Why

The dashboard should not only tell students what has happened. It should also help them understand what they can do next.

---

## 7. Give Tutors Useful Engagement Indicators

### Recommendation

Where appropriate, provide tutors with indicators such as:

- Recent activity
- Weekly activity
- Time spent
- Inactivity
- Changes in engagement
- Progress

### Why

These indicators could help tutors identify learners who may need additional support.

---

# Recommended Dashboard Approach

The dashboard should focus on a small number of clear and useful indicators rather than displaying too much information at once.

The main areas should be:

1. **My Progress**
2. **My Learning Activity**
3. **My Skills**
4. **My Achievements**
5. **My Next Step**

The aim is to make the information simple enough for students to understand quickly while still providing useful information for tutors.
