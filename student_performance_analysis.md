## Data Analysed

The analysis focused on the learners who reached the final assessment stage.

Klik2learn started with **over 100 students**, but **36 learners reached the final test** and were included in the final performance analysis.

The analysis included:

- Weekly learning activity
- Activities completed
- Time spent learning
- Mid-course performance
- Final performance
- CEFR level
- CEFR improvement
- English skills
- Numeracy activity

---

## Learner Completion

A key observation was the difference between the number of students who started the programme and the number who reached the final test.

More than **100 students started**, while **36 learners reached the final assessment**.

This highlighted the importance of looking not only at final performance, but also at **learner engagement throughout the course**.

The data could help identify patterns in engagement that occur before learners reach the final assessment.

> **Important:** The analysis does not assume why learners did not reach the final test. Reasons such as withdrawal, inactivity, course completion status or other factors would need to be confirmed from the underlying data.

---

## Engagement Analysis

I reviewed learner activity across the available learning weeks to understand how engagement changed over time.

One of the main patterns identified was that learner activity was higher during the earlier weeks and generally reduced after around **Weeks 3–4**.

This suggested that maintaining learner engagement throughout the course could be an important area for further attention.
